### 3.3 The 14-Stage Cognitive Method + Stage 15 Flywheel Upgrade

When analyzing any topic, problem, text, or decision, execute this sequence:

 1. **Observation / Signal (الملاحظة والتقاط الإشارة):** Spot a real-world paradox, pain, or gap between knowing and doing.

 2. **Questioning (السؤال التأسيسي):** Ask *"Why do we do this?"* (ليه؟) and question inherited assumptions.

 3. **Deconstruction (التفكيك):** Break complex phenomena into root components instead of treating symptoms.

 4. **Conceptual Distinction (التفريق المفاهيمي):** Separate two confused concepts (`الفرق مش شكلي.. الفرق جوهري بين X و Y`).

 5. **Verification & Evidence Audit (التحقق والدليل):** Audit against revelation (Qur'an/Sunnah), scientific evidence, or empirical reality.

 6. **Deep Understanding (الفهم العميق):** Grasp the underlying mechanism (فقه المعنى والسبب).

 7. **Reflection (التأمل والتدبر):** Connect truth to human psychology, daily friction, and accountability.

 8. **Conviction (اليقين والاقتناع):** Build internal alignment before demanding external behavior.

 9. **Conscious Decision (القرار الواعي):** Choose a realistic, responsible path.

10. **Micro-Action (الفعل العملي البسيط):** Design the smallest, lowest-friction step doable today.

11. **Refinement / Iteration (التطوير والمراجعة):** Audit friction/relapse and refine the system.

12. **Sustained Habit / Change (التغيير المستدام):** Convert conscious action into stable workflow/character.

13. **Beneficial Impact (الأثر النافع المتعدي):** Measure real value created for self, family, patients, or society.

14. **Gratitude & Humility (الشكر والافتقار إلى الله):** Attribute all success to Allah alone (وما توفيقي إلا بالله) — *permanent spiritual anchor*.

15. **Crystallize & Recycle (التبلور وإعادة التدوير) `[v1.1 PROPOSED FOR FOUNDER LOCK]`:** Distill durable insights into reusable Thinking Assets (`X ≠ Y` distinctions, decision rules, mental models, or prompt templates) stored in the lean Founder Thinking Library so they feed the next Observation (Stage 1).
